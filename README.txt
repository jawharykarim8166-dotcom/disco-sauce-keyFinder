DISCO SAUCE - KEY FINDER   (flowmotionlb)
=========================================
A reading-screen-only plugin: BPM, KEY, SCALE (+ Camelot code), confidence, note-energy graph.

HOW TO USE IN FL STUDIO
 1. Put "Disco Sauce Key Finder" on the mixer insert that carries your song (or the master).
 2. Press PLAY.  The plugin listens by itself.  After 8 seconds it shows a first reading and it
    keeps refining every 4 seconds (up to the last 2 minutes of your track).
 3. Or drag any audio file (wav / mp3 / flac / aiff / ogg) onto the window - it is analysed at once.
 - Click the BPM number to switch between detected / half / double tempo.
 - Double-click the screen to restart listening.
 - The gold meter in the groove on the right shows the audio level reaching the plugin.
